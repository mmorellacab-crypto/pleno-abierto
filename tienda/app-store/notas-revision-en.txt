2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Pleno Abierto is a free, ad-free civic information app (open source, GPL-3.0) for people interested in politics in Spain. It shows, visually and with colour-coded results, how each parliamentary group voted on each law and decree in the Spanish Congress of Deputies (Congreso de los Diputados). Official voting records are public but hard to read; the app makes them understandable at a glance and links every item to its official source. It is independent: it does not represent the Government, Parliament or any political party, and it does not recommend voting for anyone.

3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES (no setup or login needed)
- Leyes (Laws): list of laws and decrees; filter by topic (housing, work, taxes...), party or result; tap a law to see how each group voted and a link to the official text.
- Vota tú también (Vote yourself): read a neutral summary of a real law, cast your own vote, then see the real result and which groups voted like you.
- Brújula (Compass): a 45-question political orientation test adapted from established survey questionnaires such as the European Social Survey and the CIS; results compare the user's answers with how each group actually voted. Answers stay on the device.
- Mis diputados (My MPs): choose a province to see its deputies and their participation in plenary votes.
- Más (More): parties, upcoming votes with optional LOCAL notifications (no push server, no account), sources, licences and privacy policy.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES AND DATA SOURCES
- Congreso de los Diputados open data (votes, results, deputies): https://www.congreso.es/es/opendata
- Boletín Oficial del Estado (official law texts): https://www.boe.es
- El Congreso en tu mano (list of laws, topics and agenda; CC BY 4.0 licence): https://www.elcongresoentumano.es
- GitHub Pages hosts the app's public data file (https://mmorellacab-crypto.github.io/pleno-abierto/data/dataset.json), updated daily from the sources above. This is the only network request the app makes; it sends no personal data.
- No authentication, payment, analytics, advertising or AI services are used by the app. The neutral law summaries in "Vota tú también" were drafted with AI assistance during development and are shipped as static text, each linked to its official BOE source.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works identically in every region where it is available. Its content concerns the Spanish Parliament and is in Spanish. It is not distributed in mainland China.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. All content comes from official public-sector open data, reused under Spain's public-sector information reuse law (Ley 37/2007) and the sources' own reuse conditions, plus data from El Congreso en tu mano under CC BY 4.0, credited in the app (Más > Fuentes, licencias y privacidad). Party names and colours are used only to identify each group factually. Legal notice: https://mmorellacab-crypto.github.io/pleno-abierto/aviso-legal.html
